The thinking behind the pixels
<case-study />

A little context.
A lot of intention.

The challenge. Eco-travel content is usually heavy: certifications, impact numbers, itineraries. The goal was to keep the story inspiring while keeping every section easy to scan.

The approach. A single scrolling story — mission, curated destinations, the environmental roadmap, then FAQ — with generous imagery, clear section rhythm and a persistent call to action to start exploring.

  • Curated destination cards with rating and starting price
  • Environmental vision section: mangrove, plastic-neutral, socio-economic, carbon offsetting
  • FAQ accordion that answers booking and carbon-neutral questions
  • Direct WhatsApp booking with a pre-filled message
