The thinking behind the pixels
<case-study />

A little context.
A lot of intention.

The challenge. Turn a broad topic — smart energy, waste and mobility — into a place where visitors can learn, discuss and then actually do something.

The approach. A multi-page portal built around three verbs: learn, connect, act. Each page has a clear job, and interactive pieces make the impact tangible instead of abstract.

  • Multi-page structure: Home, Forum, Portfolio and Smart Tech
  • Interactive impact lab with volunteer and event sliders
  • Three-step "Eco Match" quiz that recommends an action path
  • Bilingual interface (ID / EN)
