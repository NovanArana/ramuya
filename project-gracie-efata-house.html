The thinking behind the pixels
<case-study />

A little context.
A lot of intention.

The challenge. A room listing has to feel personal and premium while answering the practical questions fast: what is included, where is it, and is a room available?

The approach. A one-page layout that moves from feeling (hero and gallery) to facts (facilities, location) to action (floor plan and WhatsApp), with the call to action always within reach.

  • Interactive floor plan — pick a floor, tap a room, ask about it on WhatsApp
  • Photo and video gallery of rooms, pool and shared spaces
  • Facilities and nearby-places sections for long-term residents
  • Search-ready: custom domain, meta description and Google verification
