A little context.
A lot of intention.

The challenge. A small bakery doesn’t need a complicated online shop — it needs to look delicious, show what’s on offer and make ordering effortless on a phone.

The approach. A light one-page site that moves from the brand’s story to the menu and straight to contact, with WhatsApp acting as the checkout and a persistent “Order Now” button.

  • One-page structure: Home, Our Story, Menu and Contact
  • Menu built around the bakery’s signature treats: risoles, donuts and bomboloni
  • Mobile-friendly navigation with an always-reachable “Order Now” button
  • Direct WhatsApp ordering, with a clear reminder to confirm availability before paying
